# Reflection 04 – Liquid Measurement & Sensor Selection

## Take-away

I learned that sensor selection depends on liquid properties, measurement requirements, and operating conditions. Calibration, filtering, and independent safety mechanisms are essential for reliable liquid-level monitoring.

## What was good?

I successfully tested continuous ultrasonic measurement, binary water detection, and IMU-based motion classification. My volume calibration produced a relative deviation of 2.36% in one independent validation test.

## What was difficult?

Foam, liquid movement, and container tilt caused unstable ultrasonic readings. I also encountered a loose power connection during the HD-38 experiment, which initially caused incorrect readings.

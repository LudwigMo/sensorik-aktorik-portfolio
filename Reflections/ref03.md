# Reflection — Module 3

## Take-away

I learned that a sensor reading is only useful when I understand its accuracy, resolution, and uncertainty. A value with more decimal places is not necessarily more accurate. My main advice is to check the reference instrument and validate a calibration at a point that was not used to create it.

## What was good?

The examples with the LDR, DS18B20, and potentiometer helped me connect measurement theory with practical applications. I understood why different sensors need different calibration methods and why errors can come from the entire measurement chain, including the ADC.

## What was difficult?

Distinguishing resolution, accuracy, and repeatability was initially difficult. I also needed to understand which instrument could serve as a suitable reference. A multimeter with a temperature probe can provide a comparison, but it is not automatically accurate enough to calibrate another sensor.

## Exploration and show stoppers

I explored the temperature function of the PCW03A multimeter and how to use its temperature probe. Its limited temperature resolution and accuracy showed me why choosing a reference matters. For a precise DS18B20 calibration, I would need a reference thermometer with better specified accuracy.

## Interaction with peers and instructors

A useful question for discussion with peers and instructors is which reference instrument is suitable for the required measurement accuracy. I would also like feedback on how to distinguish sensor response time from delays introduced by sampling and filtering.

## How did you verify AI suggestions?

I asked AI how to use the multimeter for temperature measurements and whether it could serve as a reference. I compared the explanation with the instrument's displayed mode and the specifications provided from its manual. This helped me distinguish a rough temperature comparison from a reliable calibration; agreement between two readings alone would not prove accuracy.

## Help and extra work

AI helped me understand the terminology and interpret the multimeter's limitations. I spent additional time clarifying how the temperature probe should be connected and how reference quality affects calibration. This made me more careful about accepting a displayed number as a trustworthy measurement.
